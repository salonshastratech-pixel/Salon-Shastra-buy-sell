Privacy policy

Salon Shastra Innovations Private Limited · Hyderabad, Telangana, India · Last updated {{ updated }}

Salon Shastra helps salon owners sell, grow or get support for their salons, helps investors and buyers find the right salon, and helps people find salon jobs and beauty and hair training. This page explains what information we collect when you contact us — by phone, WhatsApp, our website forms or our advertisements — and how we use it.

What we collect

  • Contact details: your name, mobile / WhatsApp number, e-mail address, city and area.
  • What you tell us about your salon or plans: for salon owners — salon details, rent, lease, staff, sales and the price you are looking for; for investors and buyers — budget, preferred areas and the kind of salon you want; for job seekers — role, experience, skills, current and expected salary; for students — courses, timing and budget.
  • Messages and calls: WhatsApp messages you send to our business number, and notes our team writes after speaking with you.
  • Documents you choose to share, such as photos of your salon or a rent agreement.

How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry, call you back and send you the forms and information you asked for.
  • To match salon owners with suitable buyers or investors, and job seekers with suitable salons.
  • To send service messages on WhatsApp (for example a call-back confirmation, a form link or a visit confirmation). Promotional messages are sent only if you agreed to receive them — reply STOP at any time and they end.
  • To keep a record of our work for you, and to meet legal and accounting duties.

Who can see it

  • Only the Salon Shastra team working on your enquiry.
  • Salon owners: your salon's name and identity are never shared with a buyer or investor without your permission. Before that, only a confidential summary without your name is shared.
  • Service providers that run our systems for us — hosting (Render), database (Supabase) and messaging (Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform, which processes WhatsApp messages under Meta's own terms). They may use the data only to provide that service.
  • We do not sell your information, and we do not share it with advertisers.

How long we keep it, and how it is protected

We keep your information for as long as we are working with you and for a reasonable time afterwards, or longer only where the law requires it (for example invoices). It is stored on secure servers, sent over encrypted connections, and only signed-in team members can see it.

Your choices

  • Ask us what information we hold about you, and ask us to correct it.
  • Ask us to delete your information — see how to delete your data.
  • Stop WhatsApp messages at any time by replying STOP.

Children

Our services are meant for adults. Students under 18 should contact us together with a parent or guardian.

Changes

If we change this policy, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
