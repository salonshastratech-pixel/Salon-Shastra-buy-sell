How to delete your data

Salon Shastra Innovations Private Limited · Last updated {{ updated }}

You can ask Salon Shastra to delete the information we hold about you at any time — your contact details, the answers you gave in our forms, notes about your enquiry and your WhatsApp conversation with our business number.

Send us your request

  1. On WhatsApp: send DELETE MY DATA to +91 86868 78090 from the number you used with us, or
  2. By e-mail: write to support@salonshastra.com with your name and the mobile number you used with us.

What happens next

  • We may call or message you once to confirm the request really comes from you.
  • We delete your information within 30 days and confirm on WhatsApp or e-mail when it is done.
  • We stop all messages to you straight away — you can also reply STOP at any time.
  • We keep only what the law requires us to keep (for example invoices for services you paid for), for as long as the law requires, and use it for nothing else.

Messages you sent on WhatsApp

Deleting your data with us does not remove messages from your own phone. Meta's handling of WhatsApp data is described in WhatsApp's privacy policy.
