Import from sheets

Drop in any Google Sheet or Excel file — sellers, buyers or both. TeamOS reads every tab, keeps only the relevant people, merges repeats and updates people already in the system. You see everything before it is saved.

The sheet must be shared as "Anyone with the link can view". Private sheets: File → Download → Microsoft Excel (.xlsx), then upload the file.

What it does for you

  • Finds the seller and buyer tabs; ignores staff lists, dashboards, dropdown lists, instructions and empty tabs.
  • Understands columns by meaning (any wording), and ignores advertising columns.
  • Cleans phones, amounts ("10–20 Lakhs", "40k"), chair bands, floors, years, cities and areas.
  • Leaves out test rows, invalid numbers, "not interested", "wrong number" and job seekers — with the reason.
  • One person = one record: repeats are merged; people already in TeamOS get only their empty fields filled.
  • New leads go to the least-busy person in the seller or buyer team, first call due next working morning.

Connected Google Sheets

checked every 10 minutes

Connect the sheets your team keeps filling (Google Form responses, ad leads). Every 10 minutes TeamOS looks for new people. Normally it prepares an import preview and you press Import now after a quick look. For ad leads (Meta instant forms → Google Sheet) switch on Auto-import: new leads come straight in, shared between the team in turn (as set in Leads & data), each with a first call due within 10 minutes.
Syncing keeps TeamOS in step with the sheet: the same person is never added twice (matched by mobile number across every sheet); only rows that changed are read; a field the team changed in the sheet is updated in TeamOS — unless someone changed it in TeamOS since the last sync, then TeamOS keeps its value and notes what the sheet says. Every change is on the record's timeline (old → new), so nothing is lost.
