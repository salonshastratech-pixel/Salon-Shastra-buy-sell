TeamOS on your phone

Add TeamOS to your home screen — it opens full-screen like an app. No app store needed.

Android (Chrome)

  1. Open TeamOS in Chrome and sign in.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu (top right).
  3. Tap Install app (or Add to Home screen).

Or tap here:

iPhone (Safari)

  1. Open TeamOS in Safari and sign in.
  2. Tap the Share button (square with an arrow).
  3. Tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.

Good to know

  • Customer details are never stored on the phone — every screen comes fresh from TeamOS, so a lost phone doesn't leak data. Sign out if you share the phone.
  • Tapping a customer's number dials it; after the call, the page jumps to Log a call.
  • Without internet you'll see a short "you're offline" notice instead of old information.
